Booking checklist

Five checks before paying for an excursion

1. Set a budget per person, including transport and optional extras. 2. Compare identical dates, duration and group sizes. 3. Read the itinerary for admission, meals and pickup details. 4. Review cancellation deadlines, accessibility and recent reviews. 5. Confirm the total at checkout before booking.

Example: calculate the real cost of a day trip

Imagine a ticket-only tour costs €45, with €20 admission and €10 transport per person. Its effective cost is €75. A €70 package including admission and transport would be €5 cheaper per traveler, provided the itineraries are comparable. These figures are illustrative, not current Viator prices.

For a family, repeat the comparison using the actual adult and child rates. Check whether the quoted amount is per person or per group, and account for any currency conversion charges.

Good to know

Questions to ask before booking

How do I compare two tours with different prices?

Divide the total group cost by the number of travelers, then compare admission, meals, pickup and duration. A lower headline price may exclude essentials.

Where should I check a Viator voucher?

Read the conditions of the specific offer and verify the final checkout amount. A universal discount is not guaranteed.

How much time should I leave between excursions?

Allow for travel to the next meeting point, possible delays and a meal break. Avoid bookings with tightly overlapping schedules.

Who handles my reservation?

This independent guide provides links to Viator. Check the booking confirmation for the operator, contact details and applicable terms.
